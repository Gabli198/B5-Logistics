About B5 Logistics

B5 Logistics began with just one or two trucks, hauling construction materials within Pampanga and Central Luzon. As demand grew from contractors and suppliers who needed dependable, on-time delivery, so did we — expanding our fleet and building a network of warehouses across Luzon and Visayas. Today, B5 Logistics stands as a trusted hauling partner for construction businesses throughout the region, but our commitment to reliability has never changed.
