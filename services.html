OUR Services

Reliable delivery, warehousing, and coverage built for construction businesses

Bulk Hauling Materials

B5 Logistics hauls a wide range of construction materials — including cement, gravel, steel bars, sand, and lumber — with dedicated trucks assigned to each job for efficient, on-schedule delivery. Once a client specifies the material, destination, and distance, our team loads, transports, and delivers directly to the site. With warehouses strategically divided across our coverage areas, we ensure every delivery stays on time. 

Warehouse & Storage Access

B5 Logistics operates 8 warehouses strategically positioned across Luzon and Visayas, allowing us to stage and organize materials closer to their final destinations. This internal network means faster dispatch times, safer handling of materials before they reach the job site, and fewer transfers that could lead to damage or delays. It's the operational backbone that keeps our deliveries fast, secure, and on schedule.

Scheduled & On-Time Delivery

Every job is assigned a dedicated truck, so there's no waiting on shared fleets or competing schedules — your delivery stays a priority from pickup to drop-off. This focused approach lets us commit to firm delivery windows and follow through on them consistently. For construction projects, that reliability means avoiding the costly delays that come from materials arriving late..
